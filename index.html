1 · Whole brands

A whole brand in one tap

Why it's there“I want to scroll every product on sale and see where it's available. I don't want to tap on every item to check.”

2 · Your own lists

A different list for different things

Why it's there“Make a list for a kind of food, one drink, a whole brand, or a mix of all three.”

Each list is a tab along the top. Swipe between them, or tap + for a new one.

3 · Linked across shops

The same product at two shops, on one row

Why it's there“Meat pies from Coles and Woolworths showed up as two different items, so I couldn't compare them. It should find the match, and then I confirm it.”

4 · Price alerts

Your phone tells you when the price gets there

Why it's there“One week the app missed some specials, and I bought things at normal price. I wanted it to tell me, like a notification.”

Alerts are off until you turn them on.

5 · Price history

Every price it has seen, on one chart

Why it's there“How will it show the price history of an item? Show the normal price too, for example on sale for $7.95, normally $10.”

Kirks Sugar Free Pasito at Coles, every check up to 1 Nov 2026.
